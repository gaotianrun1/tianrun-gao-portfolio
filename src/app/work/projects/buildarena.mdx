## Publication

Tian Xia, **Tianrun Gao**, Wenhao Deng, Long Wei, Xiaowei Qian, Jiang Yixian, Chenglei Yu, Tailin Wu.

**BuildArena: A Physics-Aligned Interactive Benchmark of LLMs for Engineering Construction.** ICML 2026.

## Why it matters

BuildArena connects agentic and generative AI systems with engineering construction problems. It is a bridge between civil engineering, physics alignment, and AI evaluation.
